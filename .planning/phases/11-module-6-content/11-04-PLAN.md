Build an interactive OMO visualization showing supply/demand for reserves and the 4-step implementation chain. Dashboard-style panel.

**Two sections:**

**Section A: Supply/Demand for Reserves (SVG chart)**
- X-axis: Quantity of reserves
- Y-axis: Federal funds rate (interest rate)
- Downward-sloping demand curve (banks demand fewer reserves at higher rates)
- Vertical supply curve (Fed controls supply)
- Equilibrium dot showing federal funds rate
- Slider: "Fed Action" — shifts supply curve left (OMO sale) or right (OMO purchase)
- Animated equilibrium movement when slider changes
- Show: "OMO purchase → supply ↑ → rate ↓" and "OMO sale → supply ↓ → rate ↑"
- NOTE: This is the POLICY perspective (how the Fed shifts supply to hit a target rate). Different from Module 5's ShortTermFundingDashboard which shows the MARKET perspective.

**Section B: 4-Step Implementation Chain**
- Step 1: FOMC decides target rate (show target range, e.g., 5.00-5.25%)
- Step 2: NY Fed manages SOMA portfolio
- Step 3: Trading desk buys/sells from Primary Dealers
- Step 4: Balance sheet impact — Assets +securities, Liabilities +reserves (newly created)
- Click each step to expand details
- FlowArrow connecting steps with animated dots
- Real example: "July 19, 2021: Fed bought $1.4B Treasury securities with 15-22 year maturity"

**Bottom insight:** "After 2008: Fed reduced target from 5.25% (mid-2007) to 0-0.25% (Dec 2008). With reserves already abundant, traditional OMOs became less effective — new tools were needed."

Use existing SVG supply/demand curve pattern from ShortTermFundingDashboard but simpler (one slider for Fed action). Use FlowArrow for implementation chain. Keep under 800 lines.
