**Timeline events (with real dates and data):**
1. **1661 Sweden** — Private bank issues paper banknotes (heavy copper coins replaced)
2. **1661-1668** — Over-issuance → bank run → collapse (animate: banknotes multiplying → doubt spreading → run)
